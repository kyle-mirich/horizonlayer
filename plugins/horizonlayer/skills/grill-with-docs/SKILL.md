---
name: grill-with-docs
description: An interview to sharpen a plan or design, which also creates docs (ADRs and glossary) as we go.
---

Interview me about every aspect of this until we reach a shared understanding, using the `/domain-modeling` skill as we go. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Ask the questions one at a time, waiting for feedback on each question before continuing.

If a *fact* can be found by exploring the environment (filesystem, tools, etc.), look it up rather than asking me. The *decisions*, though, are mine — put each one to me and wait for my answer.

As decisions land, sharpen domain terms and record them with domain-modeling: update the glossary and write ADRs/CONTEXT.md entries inline.

Do not act on it until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding.
